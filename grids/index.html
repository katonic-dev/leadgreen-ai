Grids

22 grids, one page each

Energy per token does not change with geography; carbon per token does. Each page prices the whole index on one country's grid, shows the grid's trend since 2023, and says which reporting line the figure belongs on. The 22 grids are 15 markets Katonic sells in or expects to, plus seven reference grids; all 193 areas Ember covers are in grid.json.

Basis Ember yearly emissions intensity, 2024, lifecycle, location-based, gCO2e per kWh. Ember's 2024 figures run from Ethiopia at 23.5 to Turkmenistan at 1,306.2, a spread of 55.6×. Updated 18 October 2026.

Source: Ember, Yearly electricity data, October 2026 release (CC BY 4.0), retrieved 17 October 2026. Lifecycle emissions intensity: CO2e per kWh of net generation, built from per-technology lifecycle factors (IPCC AR5 WG3 Annex III midpoints, all gases at GWP-100), so upstream methane, supply chains and plant manufacture are included. Production-based: imports and grid losses are excluded. A location-based figure for comparing places, not a Scope 2 combustion factor to file as reported.
