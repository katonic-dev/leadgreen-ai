Hungary: the carbon of AI inference per billion tokens

Hungary's grid ran at 184 gCO2e per kWh in 2024 on Ember's yearly figures, 2.6× cleaner than the world average of 473, and 46th of the 193 countries Ember covers, counting from the cleanest. A billion output tokens of the index's reference row (Qwen 3 32B, 2 × H100, 63.5 Wh/M) served on it carries about 11.7 kg CO2e of GPU-side emissions; the index's lowest row carries 2.4 kg and its highest 105.4 kg. Energy per token does not change with geography; carbon per token does.

Hungary's grid, year by year

From 2023 to 2025 the intensity fell 17 percent, from 195.6 to 162.6 gCO2e per kWh. The table uses 2024, the latest year with a value for every listed grid; on the 2025 figure the reference row would carry 10.3 kg per billion tokens.

Lifecycle emissions intensity: CO2e per kWh of net generation, built from per-technology lifecycle factors (IPCC AR5 WG3 Annex III midpoints, all gases at GWP-100), so upstream methane, supply chains and plant manufacture are included. Production-based: imports and grid losses are excluded. A location-based figure for comparing places, not a Scope 2 combustion factor to file as reported. A GPU server draws much the same power around the clock, while an annual average weights each hour by its generation, so on a grid whose clean supply peaks at midday a flat load sees a higher figure than the average.

Reporting

Which line it goes on

For GPUs you run in Hungary, the electricity is your own consumption: it goes into your ESRS E1-5 energy total and its emissions into E1-6 as Scope 2, location-based, which is the method this page's figure matches. The market-based figure nets your contracted supply and needs your own contracts. Tokens bought from a hosted API are the provider's Scope 2 and your Scope 3 category 1, and sit outside your E1-5 line; the why page lists what to ask the provider. Ember's figure is a lifecycle intensity for comparing places, not a Scope 2 combustion factor to file as reported.

Questions

Asked about AI carbon in Hungary

What is the grid carbon intensity of Hungary?

184 gCO2e per kWh in 2024, on Ember's yearly electricity data: a lifecycle, location-based figure for the country's own generation, 2.6× cleaner than the world average of 473. Ember's 2025 figure is 162.6.

How much CO2 does a million AI tokens produce in Hungary?

At the index's reference row (Qwen 3 32B on 2 × H100, 63.5 Wh per million output tokens, GPU only), about 11.7 grams of CO2e per million tokens, or 11.7 kg per billion, on Hungary's 2024 grid. Across the index the range is 2.4 to 105.4 kg per billion tokens. A lightly loaded server sits above these floors, and host, network and cooling are not counted.

Is running AI in Hungary cleaner than the world average?

Hungary's 2024 intensity of 184 gCO2e per kWh is 2.6× cleaner than the world average of 473 (473 gCO2e per kWh). The same tokens on the same GPUs produce the same energy figure anywhere; only the carbon figure moves with the grid.

Can I use this figure for my Scope 2 report in Hungary?

As a comparison, not as a filing. Ember's intensity is a lifecycle figure on net generation, not a Scope 2 combustion factor, and it is location-based; a market-based figure needs your own contracted supply. Tokens bought from a hosted API land in your Scope 3 category 1, not your Scope 2. The why page explains both.
