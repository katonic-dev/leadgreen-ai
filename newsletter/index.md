## What an edition looks like

*Sample, built from the current index*

**The number.** 63.5 Wh per million output tokens: Qwen 3 32B on 2 × H100, the index's reference row, at a running batch of 256 and 44 ms median inter-token latency.

**The note.** The same model on the same cards reads 452 Wh/M at a running batch of 8. The 7.1× between those two rows is not the model and not the hardware. It is how busy the server was.

**The pointer.** ML.ENERGY Leaderboard v3.0, Text Conversation tab, sorted by energy per token, with the About tab open beside it for the method.

**Cadence.** Monthly, on the first working day, or sooner when a measurement lands that changes a tile. **Length.** Under 200 words. **Archive.** Every edition is posted to the [blog](https://leadgreen.ai/blog/) the same day, so the RSS feed carries them too.

**Own Your AI.** Prem Naraindas's fortnightly LinkedIn newsletter on sovereign AI will carry a three-line "meter" block with one reading from this index in every issue, from its next edition. The two do not overlap: Own Your AI is about control, this one is about energy.
