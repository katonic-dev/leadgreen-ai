How to put AI energy efficiency in an RFP

Ask every bidder for the same figure at the same load: GPU energy per million output tokens (Wh/M) for each proposed model and serving configuration, measured at steady state on the proposed hardware at a stated average running batch, with median and p99 inter-token latency reported, GPU energy only, naming the method, the tool, the versions and the date. Score the answers with a gate on completeness, a floor from the index's rows for the same model and GPU, a challenge at {{CHALLENGE_TOLERANCE_PCT}} percent below the floor, and the consistency check Wh/M = average GPU power ÷ output tokens per second × 1,000,000 ÷ 3,600. The clause, the scoring box and a second clause for hosted APIs are below, ready to paste.

What to ask for

Six things every answer must carry

An energy figure without its load, its latency and its boundary cannot be compared with another bidder's. The clause asks for all six together; the scoring box treats an answer missing any of them as not stated.

The figures the clause asks every bidder for, and why each is there

Power usage effectiveness and renewable share describe the building, not the work. Keep them in the facility section of the RFP; the clause below measures the model, the GPUs and the load.

The clause

On-premise or dedicated GPUs, ready to paste

RFP clause, ready to paste

"The bidder shall state the GPU energy per million output tokens (Wh/M) for each proposed model and serving configuration, measured at steady state with the proposed serving engine on the proposed hardware, at an average running batch of [N] and with median inter-token latency not exceeding [T] ms and p99 inter-token latency not exceeding [T99] ms, both reported, on the prompt set supplied by the buyer in Annex [X], stating the average prompt and output token counts measured; where the buyer supplies none, on the LM Arena conversation sample used by the ML.ENERGY Leaderboard v3.0, named as such. Steady state, running batch and latency are as defined in the Lead Green measurement protocol v{{PROTOCOL_VERSION}} (leadgreen.ai/contribute/#protocol): a fixed cap of [N] sequences, held at least {{PROTO_MIN_MINUTES}} minutes after warm-up and for at least {{PROTO_MIN_REQUESTS}} requests; the bidder shall report both the cap and the average running batch the server actually held, and the median and p99 inter-token latency over the same window. The bidder shall name the measurement method, the sampling tool, the software versions and the date. The figure shall be GPU energy only; host, network and cooling energy, where measured, shall be reported as separate figures and not folded in. The bidder shall attach the GPU power log and serving-engine log from which the figure was computed and shall state time to first token at the same load. The buyer may repeat the measurement on the delivered system at acceptance with the same prompt set; a result more than [X] percent above the stated figure is a non-conformance. Figures for sliced (MIG) configurations shall be measured with every slice on the card serving the proposed workload at the same time, reporting the card's energy per million output tokens, the slice profile, the slice count and the slices loaded. No figure may divide the card's energy by a slice count or come from a run in which any slice sat idle. A figure derived from a whole-card measurement at a different profile is not acceptable. Any carbon figure quoted shall name the grid, the emission factor source and year, whether the factor is combustion-only or lifecycle, and whether any market-based instruments have been applied, with the location-based figure stated in all cases. A bidder that cannot measure GPU energy for the proposed service, for example a hosted API on a closed-weight model, shall state that it cannot, shall attach the model provider's published energy documentation if any (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Annex XI, Section 1, point 2(e)), and shall not substitute a per-query or per-request figure that lacks the output token count, the concurrency and the hardware. A figure withheld as confidential is treated as not stated. The bidder may mark the raw logs confidential; the figure, the method, the running batch, the latency and the inclusion statement are not confidential and will be shared across the evaluation panel."

Fill [N] with the running batch you expect in production and [T] and [T99] with the latency your users will accept. The index uses {{ITL}} ms, the leaderboard's default, as a benchmark ceiling, not a recommendation, and no measured configuration reaches it. For an interactive assistant set [T] where your users would notice, and use the same [N] and [T] for every bidder. Everything else in the clause is there so that two bidders' numbers can be compared.

Scoring

How to score the answers

Seven rules, in order. The floor comes from the index's rows for the same model and GPU, so a bidder's number is checked against a public measurement rather than against the other bidders.

Scoring the answers

  1. Gate. An answer counts only if it carries the figure, the cap and the average running batch held, median and p99 inter-token latency, average output tokens per response, output tokens per second, average GPU power summed across cards, the method, the tool, the versions, the date and the GPU-only statement. An answer missing any of them scores zero on this criterion and is not disqualified.
  2. Floor. For the same model and GPU, the floor is the configuration in models/<slug>.json with the smallest average running batch at or above [N] whose median inter-token latency is at or under [T] ms; if there is none, the configuration with the highest measured running batch.
  3. Tolerance. An answer more than {{CHALLENGE_TOLERANCE_PCT}} percent below the floor is challenged, and the bidder supplies the raw power and engine logs. The protocol's own repeat threshold is {{REPEAT_THRESHOLD_PCT}} percent.
  4. Consistency. Wh/M must equal average GPU power ÷ output tokens per second × 1,000,000 ÷ 3,600. On the reference row: {{CONS_W}} W ÷ {{CONS_TPS}} tokens per second × 1,000,000 ÷ 3,600 = {{CONS_WH}} Wh/M. An answer that fails the check beyond rounding is treated as not stated.
  5. Cannot measure. An answer that declares it cannot measure, as the clause provides, is scored as not stated, not compared.
  6. Confidential. A figure withheld as confidential is scored as not stated. Logs may be confidential; the figure, method, running batch, latency and inclusion statement may not.
  7. Where Katonic bids. Lead Green is a Katonic project. In a tender where Katonic may bid, take floor values from the ML.ENERGY rows directly and disclose the compilation, using the wording in Using the index in a tender.

Hosted API

If the lot is a hosted or shared inference service

The index cannot measure a hosted API: its fleet is closed, its batches mix every customer's traffic and it states no concurrency. The clause below asks the provider for the fleet-average figure and the facts behind it, and the index tells the buyer whether the first answer is plausible. The why page has the seven questions to ask in writing and the reporting line the tokens land on.

RFP clause, hosted or shared inference service

"For a hosted or shared inference service, the bidder shall state, for each proposed model and service tier, the fleet-average GPU energy per million output tokens (Wh/M) over the preceding quarter at the fleet's actual utilisation, and shall state whether host, network and cooling energy are included; where they are measured they shall be reported as separate figures and not folded in. The bidder shall name the facilities and grid regions that will serve the buyer's traffic and the grid or contracted supply behind each, shall state their power usage effectiveness and the host-to-GPU power ratio of the serving systems, and shall state the rule by which the energy of a shared fleet is allocated to the buyer. Any carbon figure quoted shall name the grid, the emission factor source and year, whether the factor is combustion-only or lifecycle, and whether any market-based instruments have been applied, with the location-based figure stated in all cases. Any per-prompt or per-request figure offered shall state the output length, the concurrency and the boundary behind it. The bidder shall restate these figures to the buyer every quarter for the term of the contract. A bidder that cannot state the fleet figure shall say so; that answer is scored as not stated, not compared."

Use it as the second lot beside the on-premise clause above, so a tender with both lots scores both. The cannot-measure and confidential rules in "Scoring the answers" apply to it too.
